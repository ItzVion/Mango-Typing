ABOUT MANGOTYPING

Typing practice for everyone.

MangoTyping is an independent typing-practice project designed to make improving keyboard skills approachable. The website offers free typing tests, printable practice sheets, typing games, and guided tutor lessons.

Use the tools at your own pace, practise regularly, and review saved results when signed in. Speed and accuracy measurements are practice feedback, not a guarantee of any particular outcome.
