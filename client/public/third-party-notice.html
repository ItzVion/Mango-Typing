Third-party services notice

MangoTyping is an independent project. Certain features rely on external service providers. Information is sent to a provider when needed to operate the feature or infrastructure it supplies, subject to the provider's terms, privacy practices, and applicable law. Provider roles may change as the site evolves; this page describes the services currently known to be integrated into the website code.

Google Identity Services

Optional sign-in

If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides an identity credential that MangoTyping verifies on its server. The app may receive the Google account identifier, verified email, and available profile name/photo. This sign-in flow does not give MangoTyping your Google password and does not request Gmail or Google Drive content access.

Razorpay

Optional donation payments

When donation checkout is enabled and you choose to contribute, Razorpay processes payment credentials. MangoTyping receives transaction details such as amount, order/payment identifiers, status, and timestamps for reconciliation and support. MangoTyping does not intentionally store full card numbers, CVV/CVC, UPI PINs, or banking passwords.

Razorpay Privacy Policy ↗

Neon

Database infrastructure

Neon provides managed PostgreSQL database infrastructure used to store application records, including account/profile records, typing history, settings, donation metadata, and legal-page content.

Neon Privacy Policy ↗

Vercel

Website hosting and deployment

Vercel hosts and delivers the website and runs its API deployment. As part of hosting, security, and operations, the platform may process request metadata and operational logs.

Email/SMTP services

Verification and account messages

The project can use the SMTP/email provider configured in its settings to send verification codes and account notices. A provider that handles these messages may process recipient addresses and message delivery metadata.
